Race results

Each paddler gets two runs. A run's total is its time plus penalty seconds. Your result is the better of your two run totals.

  • Paddlers are ranked on their best total within each class (C1M, C1W, K1M, K1W).
  • A run marked DNF (did not finish) or DNS (did not start) has no total. If one run is complete, the other run counts.
  • If both runs are DNF or DNS, the paddler is listed at the bottom without a rank and gets no points.
  • Paddlers with exactly the same best total share the rank, shown as “=2”. The next place is skipped (1, =2, =2, 4).
  • The highlighted run is the one that counted. “Behind” is the gap to the winner's best total.

Race points

In each class at each race, the winner gets 1000 points and every place after that gets an equal share less:

points = 1000 − (rank − 1) × 1000 ÷ N, rounded to the nearest whole number.

  • N is the number of ranked paddlers in the class, with a minimum of 5, so small classes don't give away big gaps for single places. With 3 finishers, points are 1000, 800, 600. With 8 finishers they are 1000, 875, 750, 625, 500, 375, 250, 125.
  • Paddlers who share a rank also share its points.
  • Paddlers who are not ranked (both runs DNF/DNS) get 0 points and don't count towards N.

Series standings

  • Your series total in a class is the sum of your points from every race in that class. All races count.
  • Missing a race scores 0 for that race.
  • Paddlers with the same total share the rank.

Combined standings

The men's (C1M + K1M) and women's (C1W + K1W) combined standings average the two class totals:

combined = (C1 total + K1 total) ÷ 2, rounded to the nearest whole number.

If you only race one class, the other counts as 0, so paddling both classes is rewarded.

When points are published

Points are calculated once after each race and published with the results. Points for past races don't change when new results are added.
